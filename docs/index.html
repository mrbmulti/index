Mr Bmulti is the creative and educational work of Christopher Patrick Cotton, bringing songs, learning resources, science, software, art, and questions together.

Most importantly: keep the curiosity, and keep asking for evidence.

Funding is very welcome, as I am a tourist on this planet. With funding to bring in some assistance, there will be 1,000 songs, more than 20 books, a peer-to-peer sharing app for media, and many more public applications and fun learning resources.
